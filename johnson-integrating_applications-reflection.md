### **Reflection on Technical Concepts Learned**  

##1. Learning Outcomes
Throughout this project, I’ve learned a lot about software design principles, especially when it comes to building scalable, maintainable, and testable applications. By following best practices like SOLID principles, separation of concerns, and dependency injection, I’ve seen firsthand how a well-structured codebase makes a huge difference in both development and testing.  

One of the biggest takeaways was the importance of maintainability. Breaking the project into three separate backend applications—the Task Application, Weather Application, and Employee Directory—made it easier to manage and scale. Designing clear API contracts also ensured smooth communication between different parts of the system. In addition, implementing structured exception handling and logging helped improve debugging and made the system more reliable.  

Overall, this project gave me hands-on experience in real-world software development and deepened my understanding of testing methodologies. It also showed me how important it is to integrate testing early in the development process. Looking ahead, I’d love to explore areas like CI/CD integration and performance testing to further improve automation and scalability.

##2. Challenges Faces
One of the biggest challenges during the research and planning process was ensuring that the application design aligned with good software principles while also being testable, scalable, and maintainable. Striking the right balance between modularity and integration was tricky, especially when designing multiple backend services that needed to communicate efficiently. Deciding how to structure the APIs and database while keeping the system loosely coupled took careful planning.  

Despite these challenges, overcoming them provided a deeper understanding of software architecture, automated testing strategies, and best practices in debugging and logging. Each difficulty helped refine the overall project approach, making the final implementation stronger and more effective.

##3. Personal Growth
This project is one that has taught me a lot about using these tools to build applications, as well as how to keep the best practices and design principles in mind when building something more intricate than a normal lab.
